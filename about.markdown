<h1>About Wordle Words</h1>
<hr>

<p>Wordle Words answers one question quickly: <strong>is this a valid Wordle word?</strong> Every one of the
14,855 five-letter words that Wordle accepts as a guess has its own page, along with its meaning where one is available.</p>

<h2>Where the word list comes from</h2>
<p>The list matches the words accepted by the official Wordle game. We last checked it against the game on
3 November 2026. If a word has a page here, Wordle accepts it as a guess. Not every accepted guess can be a daily
answer, because the answer pool is a much smaller set of common words.</p>

<h2>Where the definitions come from</h2>
<p>Definitions come from <a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/" rel="nofollow">Wiktionary</a> through the
<a href="https://dictionaryapi.dev/" rel="nofollow">Free Dictionary API</a>, and are available under the
<a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/" rel="license nofollow">CC BY-SA</a> license.
Some rare words have no definition yet.</p>
